# Mueller and Oppenheimer 2014

Mueller, P. A., & Oppenheimer, D. M. (2014). The pen is mightier than the keyboard: Advantages of longhand over laptop note taking. *Psychological Science*, 25(6), 1159–1168.

## Claim

Students who take notes by hand learn concepts better than students who type, because typing invites copying the lecture word for word instead of processing it.

## Method

Three experiments with university students. Students watched recorded talks and took notes by hand or on a laptop, then answered factual and conceptual questions. One experiment told laptop users not to copy word for word; another let students review their notes a week later before the test.

## Results

- Laptop users wrote more and copied more of the talk word for word
- On factual questions, the groups did about the same
- On conceptual questions, students who wrote by hand did better
- Telling laptop users not to copy verbatim didn't stop them

## What I take from it

The study that started the debate, and the one most often cited for banning laptops. The verbatim result is strong. The test score difference rests on fairly small groups, which is why the replications matter: see [Urry et al 2021](Urry%20et%20al%202021.md) and [Morehead et al 2019](Morehead%20et%20al%202019.md).
