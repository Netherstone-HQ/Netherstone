# Start here

This vault is a sample world for a story or a game. Every place, person and event is its own shard, and they point to each other with @ links, so you can follow a thread from a town to the family that founded it.

## Places

Start with [The Salt Coast](Places/The%20Salt%20Coast.md), then open [Harrowgate](Places/Harrowgate.md) to see how a place shard is laid out. The Map of the coast drawing shows where they sit.

## People

Each person has a few lines on who they are, what they want, what they hide and where they live. [Mara Vell](People/Mara%20Vell.md) links to Harrowgate, and she shows up in its Linked from list. Families get a shard of their own, like [The Vell family](People/The%20Vell%20family.md).

## History

[The founding](History/The%20founding.md) tells how the coast towns began. The Timeline drawing runs from then to the day your story starts.

## Words

[Languages](Languages.md) keeps the coast's own words in one place, so names stay consistent as the world grows.

## Making it yours

Rename, rewrite or delete anything here. When you add a place or a person, copy the headings from one that already exists, and link it to at least one other shard.
