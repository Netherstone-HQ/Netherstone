# Mara Vell

Head of [The Vell family](The%20Vell%20family.md) and owner of the salt pans that keep [Harrowgate](../Places/Harrowgate.md) alive.

## Who she is

She's forty-six, sharp and tired. She took over the pans at twenty-two when her father drowned, and she has spent every year since proving she deserved them. She knows every worker by name and every debt by heart.

## What she wants

To hand the pans to her daughter intact. That gets harder each year: the inland towns have found cheaper salt in the south, and the family's old promises are coming due.

## What she hides

Her father did not drown by accident. She has suspected for years that his boat was lured onto the rocks at [Wrecker's Point](../Places/Wrecker%27s%20Point.md), and that [Old Tobin](Old%20Tobin.md) knows who lit the false lamp.

## Where she is

Lives in the Vell house above the harbour in Harrowgate. Spends most mornings on the pans and most evenings over the ledgers.
