# Start here

This vault keeps a course of study in one place. Each subject has a folder, and each topic in it is a shard: what you need to know, then a few questions to test yourself.

## Subjects

- Biology: [Cells](Biology/Cells.md), [Photosynthesis](Biology/Photosynthesis.md) and [Respiration](Biology/Respiration.md), plus the Cell diagram and Energy cycle drawings
- History: [The French Revolution](History/The%20French%20Revolution.md), [Causes of the revolution](History/Causes%20of%20the%20revolution.md) and [The Terror](History/The%20Terror.md)

Replace them with your own subjects, or keep them as examples of how a topic shard is laid out.

## How a topic shard works

- **Key points** first, in your own words, short enough to reread in a minute
- **Links** to the topics it depends on, so revising one leads you to the next
- **Test yourself** last: questions you answer from memory before looking up

[How to revise](How%20to%20revise.md) explains why answering questions works better than rereading.

## Adding a subject

Make a folder, add one shard per topic, and link each new topic to one you already have. When two subjects meet, like respiration and exercise, link across folders too.
