# Question

Do students learn more from a lecture when they take notes by hand than when they type them on a laptop?

## Why it matters

Most students now type their notes. If handwriting leads to better learning, that's a cheap change with a real payoff. If it doesn't, banning laptops in class costs students a useful tool for nothing.

## What would count as an answer

- Studies that compare the two directly, with students randomly assigned to each
- Learning measured with a test, not by asking students how well they think they did
- Results that hold up when other researchers repeat the study

## What's out of scope

Distraction from other apps during class. It matters, but it's a separate question from the note-taking itself.
