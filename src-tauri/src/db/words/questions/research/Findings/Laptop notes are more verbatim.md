# Laptop notes are more verbatim

Students who type take longer notes and copy more of the lecture word for word than students who write by hand.

## Evidence

- Found in the original study: [Mueller and Oppenheimer 2014](../Sources/Mueller%20and%20Oppenheimer%202014.md)
- Repeated in a direct replication with a larger sample: [Urry et al 2021](../Sources/Urry%20et%20al%202021.md)
- Asking laptop users not to copy verbatim didn't change much in the original study

## How sure

Fairly sure. It shows up in both studies listed above, and it makes sense: most people type faster than they write, so there's less pressure to summarise.

## What it doesn't show

That verbatim notes hurt learning. That's a separate claim, covered in [Test scores](Test%20scores.md).
