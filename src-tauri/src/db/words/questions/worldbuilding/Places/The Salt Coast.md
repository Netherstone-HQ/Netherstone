# The Salt Coast

A long cold shoreline of salt flats, low cliffs and shallow bays in the far north.

<img src="_attachments/Map of the coast.png" data-dark-src="_attachments/Map of the coast-dark.png" alt="Map of the coast" width="480" data-excalidraw="Map of the coast.excalidraw" />

## What it is like

Flat grey water, flat grey land and a sky that rarely clears. Summers are short and bright, when the pans dry and everyone works. Winters are long, and the sea fog can sit on the coast for a week.

## Who lives here

Salt workers, fishers and the families who own the pans. Almost everyone lives in [Harrowgate](Harrowgate.md); a few lamp keepers and hermits live along the cliffs, like [Old Tobin](../People/Old%20Tobin.md).

## Places

- [Harrowgate](Harrowgate.md), the only real town
- [Wrecker's Point](Wrecker%27s%20Point.md), the headland at the mouth of the bay
- The salt pans, cut into the flats east of town

## History

Settled at [The founding](../History/The%20founding.md). Before that, inland people called it the Empty Shore and avoided it.
