# Wrecker's Point

A high headland at the mouth of the bay, the first land a ship sees coming into [Harrowgate](Harrowgate.md).

## What it is like

Bare rock, wind and a single lamp house. Below the cliff, a reef that only shows at low tide. Graves from the first winter of [The founding](../History/The%20founding.md) line the path to the top.

## Who lives here

Only [Old Tobin](../People/Old%20Tobin.md), who keeps the light.

## Why the name

The reef has taken more ships than anyone has counted. The town says that is the sea's doing. The older fishers say some of those ships were lured in by a false lamp, and that the drowned coin they carried paid for half the houses in Harrowgate.

## History

The Vell heads of house swear their oath here (see [The Vell family](../People/The%20Vell%20family.md)). [Mara Vell](../People/Mara%20Vell.md)'s father was lost on the reef twenty-four years ago.
