Netherstone keeps your documents, drawings and files in a folder on your phone, called a vault. Documents are saved as Markdown.

The vault can live in the app's own storage or in a shared folder like Documents, where other apps can open it.

<ul>
<li>Format text from a toolbar above the keyboard: lists, links, headings and colors</li>
<li>Add pictures, video, audio and files, or take a photo</li>
<li>Record voice notes</li>
<li>Link documents together, tag them and view their outline</li>
<li>Make drawings and diagrams, in their own file or inside a document</li>
<li>Search the vault and filter by tags, images, drawings or voice notes</li>
<li>Restore earlier versions and deleted documents</li>
<li>Export as a PDF, a Word file or a web page</li>
<li>Share text, links and files to Netherstone from other apps</li>
</ul>

Netherstone also runs on Windows, macOS and Linux, with optional sync between devices.

Netherstone has an optional AI assistant, disabled by default. It answers questions about your documents and suggests edits for you to approve, through a cloud provider or your own server.

The code is public under GPL-3.0.
