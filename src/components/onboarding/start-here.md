# Start here

Welcome to Netherstone. A shard is a Markdown document on your computer, and your vault is the folder that holds them. A drawing is a sketch or diagram you can place in any shard. Sync keeps your vault the same on every device.

This is your first shard. Edit it, move it, or delete it once you've had a look around.

## Writing

Just start typing. Type **/** at the start of a line to add a heading, a list, a to-do, a table, a callout and more. Select text to format it.

## Finding things

Press **Ctrl+K** (**⌘K** on a Mac) to search every shard in your vault at once.

## Drawing

Switch to **Canvas** in the bar on the far left to sketch ideas, diagrams and maps.

## Your files are yours

Everything lives in this folder as ordinary files. Open them in any other app, copy them anywhere, and they're still yours.

## Sync

Sync is optional. It keeps your vault the same on every device and saves every earlier version of your files. It works through a free GitHub account, and only you can see your vault there. Turn it on with the **Sync** button at the top of the sidebar.
