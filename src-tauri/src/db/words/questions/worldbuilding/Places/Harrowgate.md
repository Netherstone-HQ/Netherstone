# Harrowgate

The harbour town of [The Salt Coast](The%20Salt%20Coast.md), built where a river meets a sheltered bay. Its name comes from the harrowing of salt pans (see [Languages](../Languages.md)).

## What it is like

Stone houses stacked up a steep hill, all facing the harbour. Salt crusts on every windowsill. The smell is fish and woodsmoke in winter, brine in summer.

## Who lives here

About two thousand people. Most work the pans or the boats. [The Vell family](../People/The%20Vell%20family.md) live in the big house at the top of the hill, where they can see every ship that comes in.

## Notable places

- The Vell house, above the harbour
- The salt sheds on the east quay, where the carts load
- The Greywater, a tavern for pan workers

## History

Founded by Ansel Vell at [The founding](../History/The%20founding.md). It has never been attacked, mostly because nobody else wants it.

## Hooks

- A salt cart came back from inland empty, its driver missing
- Someone has been buying up the Vells' old debts
- The boat [Mara Vell](../People/Mara%20Vell.md)'s father sailed has washed up again, twenty-four years after it sank at [Wrecker's Point](Wrecker%27s%20Point.md)
