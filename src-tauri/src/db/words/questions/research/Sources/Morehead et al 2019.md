# Morehead et al 2019

Morehead, K., Dunlosky, J., & Rawson, K. A. (2019). How much mightier is the pen than the keyboard for note-taking? A replication and extension of Mueller and Oppenheimer (2014). *Educational Psychology Review*, 31(3), 753–780.

## Claim

The advantage of handwriting reported by [Mueller and Oppenheimer 2014](Mueller%20and%20Oppenheimer%202014.md) is much smaller and less reliable than first thought.

## Method

A replication of the original setup with recorded talks, plus a second experiment that added digital pens (eWriters) as a third way of taking notes.

## Results

- Few reliable differences in test performance between handwriting and laptop groups
- The original pattern on conceptual questions didn't clearly come back

## What I take from it

The first serious check on the original study, and it mostly didn't find the effect. Read together with [Urry et al 2021](Urry%20et%20al%202021.md), which reached a similar result with a direct replication.
