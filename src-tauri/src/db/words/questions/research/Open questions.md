# Open questions

What the sources here don't answer yet.

- **Real courses.** Every study here tests students on a single short talk. Does anything change over a term, with exams weeks later?
- **Review.** The original study found handwriting helped when students could study their notes before a later test. Is the benefit in taking notes or in reviewing them? See [Mueller and Oppenheimer 2014](Sources/Mueller%20and%20Oppenheimer%202014.md)
- **Digital pens.** [Morehead et al 2019](Sources/Morehead%20et%20al%202019.md) tried digital pens. Do tablets with a stylus behave like paper or like a keyboard?
- **Different subjects.** Talks in these studies were general interest. Would maths or diagrams, which are hard to type, show a bigger gap?

Answering any of these would sharpen [Test scores](Findings/Test%20scores.md).
