# The Vell family

The founding family of [Harrowgate](../Places/Harrowgate.md). They have owned the salt pans since [The founding](../History/The%20founding.md), and most of the town owes them money, work or both.

## Members

- [Mara Vell](Mara%20Vell.md), head of the family
- Wren Vell, Mara's daughter, nineteen, wants to leave the coast
- Edrin Vell, Mara's cousin, runs the salt carts inland and thinks the pans should have been his

## Customs

- The head of house swears an oath in Old Inland on the headland at [Wrecker's Point](../Places/Wrecker%27s%20Point.md) (see [Languages](../Languages.md))
- Every Vell child works one summer on the pans, raking salt with the workers
- The family never sells land, only leases it

## Trouble

Ansel Vell promised the other founding families a share of the pans "when the coast is safe". Nobody ever agreed on what safe meant. Their descendants still bring it up at every bad harvest.
