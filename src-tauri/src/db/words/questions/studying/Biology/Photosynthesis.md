# Photosynthesis

Plants make their own food using light energy. It happens in the chloroplasts (see [Cells](Cells.md)) and is the reverse of [Respiration](Respiration.md).

## Key points

carbon dioxide + water → glucose + oxygen

6CO₂ + 6H₂O → C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂

- Light energy is absorbed by chlorophyll, the green pigment in chloroplasts
- It's endothermic: energy is taken in from the surroundings
- Carbon dioxide comes in through the leaves, water through the roots

**Limiting factors**: the rate is held back by whichever of these is in shortest supply.

- Light intensity
- Carbon dioxide concentration
- Temperature (too hot and the enzymes stop working)
- Amount of chlorophyll

**What the plant does with the glucose**

- Uses it in respiration
- Stores it as starch
- Turns it into cellulose for cell walls
- Combines it with nitrates to make amino acids, then proteins
- Turns it into fats and oils for storage
