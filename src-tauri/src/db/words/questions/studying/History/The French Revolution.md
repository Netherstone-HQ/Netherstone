# The French Revolution

Between 1789 and 1799, France went from an absolute monarchy to a republic, through the Terror, and ended with Napoleon in power. Start with [Causes of the revolution](Causes%20of%20the%20revolution.md).

## Key dates

| Date | Event |
| --- | --- |
| May 1789 | Estates-General meets at Versailles for the first time since 1614 |
| 20 June 1789 | Tennis Court Oath: the Third Estate swears not to part until France has a constitution |
| 14 July 1789 | Storming of the Bastille |
| August 1789 | Feudal privileges abolished; Declaration of the Rights of Man and of the Citizen |
| October 1789 | Women's march on Versailles brings the king to Paris |
| June 1791 | The royal family's flight to Varennes fails |
| September 1792 | Monarchy abolished, France declared a republic |
| 21 January 1793 | Louis XVI executed |
| 1793 to 1794 | [The Terror](The%20Terror.md) |
| 1795 to 1799 | The Directory governs |
| November 1799 | Napoleon Bonaparte seizes power |

## Key points

- The revolution moved in stages: a reform of the monarchy (1789 to 1791), then a republic, then a radical phase, then a reaction against it
- Each stage was pushed by a different group: lawyers and the middle class, then Paris crowds, then the Jacobins
- War with Austria and Prussia from 1792 made the revolution more radical and more fearful of traitors
