# Cells

All living things are made of cells. Animal and plant cells are eukaryotic: their DNA is inside a nucleus. Bacteria are prokaryotic: no nucleus.

## Key points

**In animal and plant cells**

| Part | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Nucleus | Holds the DNA, which controls what the cell does |
| Cell membrane | Controls what goes in and out |
| Cytoplasm | Gel where most of the cell's reactions happen |
| Mitochondria | Where aerobic [Respiration](Respiration.md) releases energy |
| Ribosomes | Where proteins are made |

**Only in plant cells**

| Part | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Cell wall | Made of cellulose, keeps the cell's shape |
| Chloroplasts | Hold chlorophyll and carry out [Photosynthesis](Photosynthesis.md) |
| Permanent vacuole | Filled with cell sap, keeps the cell firm |

**Bacteria** have a cell wall, membrane, cytoplasm and ribosomes, but their DNA is a single loop floating in the cytoplasm, often with extra small rings called plasmids.
