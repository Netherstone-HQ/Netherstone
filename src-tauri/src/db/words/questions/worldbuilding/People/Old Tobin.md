# Old Tobin

Keeper of the light at [Wrecker's Point](../Places/Wrecker%27s%20Point.md). Nobody in [Harrowgate](../Places/Harrowgate.md) remembers anyone else holding the lampright.

## Who he is

He's somewhere past seventy, half deaf and stubborn. He talks to the gulls and to the dead buried on the headland, and he says both answer more sensibly than the living.

## What he wants

To be left alone, and to die before anyone asks him about the night [Mara Vell](Mara%20Vell.md)'s father was lost.

## What he knows

He saw a second lamp burning on the cliff that night, below his own. He knows whose hand held it, and he has said nothing for twenty-four years because that person paid for his silence and still could ruin him.

## Where he is

The lamp house on the point. He comes into town once a month for oil and bread, and leaves before dark.
