# Respiration

Every living cell releases energy from glucose all the time. Aerobic respiration happens in the mitochondria (see [Cells](Cells.md)). It's the reverse of [Photosynthesis](Photosynthesis.md).

## Key points

**Aerobic**, with oxygen:

glucose + oxygen → carbon dioxide + water

C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂ → 6CO₂ + 6H₂O

**Anaerobic**, without enough oxygen:

- In muscles: glucose → lactic acid
- In plants and yeast: glucose → ethanol + carbon dioxide (fermentation, used for bread and beer)

**Energy**

- Respiration is exothermic: it releases energy
- Aerobic releases much more energy per glucose than anaerobic
- During hard exercise, muscles respire anaerobically and lactic acid builds up. The extra oxygen needed afterwards to break it down is the oxygen debt
